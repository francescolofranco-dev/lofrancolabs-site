Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers apps developed and published by Francesco Lo Franco under Lofranco Labs, including Gym Tracker (dev.lofrancolabs.gymtracker) and WordForge (dev.lofrancolabs.wordforge).

Data Collection & Analytics

Our apps are designed with an offline-first philosophy. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. We do not use third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics services.

Local Storage

All application data (such as workout sessions, vocabulary logs, quiz history, and personal preferences) is stored locally on your device. Android automatic cloud backups are disabled, meaning your data remains strictly on your device.

Optional Cloud Backups (Google Drive)

Where supported (such as Gym Tracker's optional Google Drive backup), the app requests access only to the restricted drive.appdata scope. Backups are saved directly into a hidden, private folder in your own Google Drive account. The developer never has access to or receives these files.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our apps, please contact lofranco.francesco@gmail.com.
