Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers apps developed and published by Francesco Lo Franco under Lofranco Labs, including Drift (dev.lofrancolabs.drift), Gym Tracker (dev.lofrancolabs.gymtracker) and WordForge (dev.lofrancolabs.wordforge).

Data Collection & Analytics

Our apps are designed with an offline-first philosophy. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. We do not use third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics services.

Local Storage

All application data (such as Drift favourites and saved listening positions, workout sessions, vocabulary logs, quiz history, and personal preferences) is stored locally on your device. Android automatic cloud backups are disabled, meaning your data remains strictly on your device.

Drift Listening Data

Drift is an offline sleep-story listening app. It stores your favourite stories and your last played story and playback position locally on your device so you can return to your saved place. This listening data is not sent to Lofranco Labs or shared with third parties. Drift has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or cloud sync, and does not offer Google Drive backup. Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfer of Drift's app data are disabled.

You can remove a favourite in the app. To delete all Drift listening data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall Drift.

Optional Cloud Backups (Google Drive)

Where supported (such as Gym Tracker's optional Google Drive backup), the app requests access only to the restricted drive.appdata scope. Backups are saved directly into a hidden, private folder in your own Google Drive account. The developer never has access to or receives these files.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
